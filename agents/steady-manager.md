# Steady manager

## Declared strategy

The Steady Manager optimizes for weekly lineup reliability and playoff qualification. It prefers secure workload, durable roles, balanced roster construction, and measured use of waiver budget.

When choices are close, prefer the player with the clearer opportunity and lower risk of leaving a starting slot unusable. Preserve enough bench coverage to absorb injuries and bye weeks.

This strategy changes judgment only. It receives the same model, data, schedule, compute budget, permissions, validator, and executor as the other manager.
